# NYC Food Safety Pulse — Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard giving a citywide snapshot of NYC restaurant health inspections — volume, grading, violations, and cuisine-level breakdowns.

## Overview

This dashboard analyzes **285K+ food safety inspections** across New York City to surface patterns in inspection activity, grading outcomes, and violation types by borough and cuisine.

## Key Insights

- **Manhattan leads inspection volume** with 105K inspections, more than double Brooklyn (74K) and Queens (70K)
- **Bangladeshi cuisine recorded the lowest average score** among all cuisine types tracked
- **Average inspection score fell** from 23.87 last year to 25 this year
- The most frequent violation citywide is **non-food contact surface not properly maintained** (36K occurrences), followed by improper employee hygiene practices (24K)
- **Critical violations outweigh non-critical ones** in every borough except Staten Island, with Manhattan showing the highest split (56K critical vs. 48K not critical)
- Grade **A** dominates outcomes across all five boroughs, with Manhattan awarding the most A grades (37K)

## Dashboard Sections

- **KPI cards** — total inspections, lowest-scoring cuisine, year-over-year average score
- **Inspection Count per Borough** — bar chart comparing inspection volume across the five boroughs
- **Grade Distribution Across NYC** — A/B/C grade breakdown per borough
- **Top 10 Most Frequent Violations** — ranked violation types citywide
- **Comparison of Violation Severity** — critical vs. non-critical violations by borough
- **Most Common Inspection Types** — inspection category breakdown
- **Cuisine Description Matrix** — grade distribution (A–Z) by cuisine type

## Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop** — data modeling, DAX measures, and dashboard visualization
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
